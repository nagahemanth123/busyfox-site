Data deletion

How to disconnect an account and have your data removed. One email is enough.

How to askBusy Fox InsightsFoxCutThis websiteWhat gets deletedHow long it takesContact

How to ask

  1. Email admin@busyfox.in with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your account, and tell us which product it is about: Busy Fox Insights, FoxCut, or agency work.
  3. We will confirm that we received it, delete the data, and write back when it is done.

Email is the only route. You do not need to fill in a form or explain why.

Busy Fox Insights

You can stop Busy Fox Insights from reaching your accounts yourself at any time, without waiting for us:

  • Facebook: Settings and privacy, then Settings, then Business integrations. Remove Busy Fox Insights.
  • Instagram: Settings, then Apps and websites. Remove Busy Fox Insights.
  • LinkedIn: Settings, then Data privacy, then Permitted services. Remove Busy Fox Insights.

Or email admin@busyfox.in. We disconnect the account and confirm by email.

Removing Busy Fox Insights on the platform stops new data from reaching us. To also erase what we already hold, such as stored tokens, scheduled posts and report figures for your accounts, send the email described above.

FoxCut

Your projects and footage live on your own device. Delete them there, or uninstall the app, and they are gone. If you created a FoxCut account, email us and we will delete the account and the details held with it.

This website

The website keeps nothing about you on our side. The only thing it saves is your motion setting, in your own browser. Clear your browser's site data for busyfox.in to remove it.

What gets deleted

  • access tokens and connection details for your social accounts;
  • profile, post and performance data collected from those accounts;
  • your account details in our products;
  • project material we hold for agency work, if you ask for that as well.

We keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as invoices and tax records, and backups that expire on their normal schedule.

How long it takes

We act on a request within 30 days and confirm by email when it is complete. Posts that were already published stay on the platform they were published to. You can remove those there.
