Who we are

Busy Fox is the trading name of Busyfox Marketing LLP, a Limited Liability Partnership based in Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh, India. We run a marketing agency, make the FoxCut video app, and operate Busy Fox Insights, the service we use to connect to and publish on client accounts. This policy covers the website busyfox.in, FoxCut and Busy Fox Insights.

The contact for anything in this policy is admin@busyfox.in.

This website

This website does not ask you to create an account, has no forms, and does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

  • Email links. The only way to contact us from the site is an email link. If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us, and we use it to reply and to do the work you ask for.
  • Motion setting. If you use the "Motion" switch in the footer, your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device.
  • Demos. The interactive demos on the site run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Hosting. Like any website, the company that hosts these pages may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.

FoxCut

FoxCut is a video editing app. It is in early access.

  • Your footage. FoxCut works on the videos, audio and images you add to it. Editing happens on your device. Your footage is not uploaded to us unless you use a feature that clearly says it sends something, and then only what that feature needs.
  • Speech. To cut pauses and write captions, FoxCut downloads a speech model to your device the first time you use it and runs it there.
  • Account and plan. If you sign in or buy a plan, we hold the details needed for that, such as your name, email address and plan. Payments are handled by a payment provider. We do not store card numbers.
  • Diagnostics. The app may send crash and error reports, with technical details such as app version and operating system, so that we can fix problems. These reports are not meant to include your footage.

Busy Fox Insights

Busy Fox Insights is how we connect a client's Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn accounts, publish what they approve and report back. Busy Fox operates it for its clients. You connect an account through a link we send you, by signing in on the official Meta or LinkedIn screen, which shows what Busy Fox Insights is asking for. We never receive your password.

With your permission, Busy Fox Insights receives:

  • your name, profile picture and the list of Pages and accounts you choose to connect;
  • posts and media on those Pages and accounts;
  • permission to publish to them;
  • performance figures such as reach, impressions, engagement and follower counts.

Only if you ask us to, it also receives:

  • comments and messages on the connected accounts, so that we can reply to your customers for you;
  • ad account data, so that we can run and report on ads for you.

We use this only to see which accounts are connected, publish the posts you approved, and build the reports we send you. We do not post without your approval, we do not sell this data, and we do not use it to advertise to you. A fuller description is on the Insights page.

You can disconnect at any time in your Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn settings, or by emailing us. We disconnect the account and confirm by email. See data deletion.

Client work

When you hire the agency, we handle the material needed for your project: your contact details, brand files, footage, photographs and account access you give us. We use it only for your work and keep it confidential.

How we use information

  • to reply to you and deliver the work or product you asked for;
  • to run, secure and fix our products;
  • to send invoices and keep the records the law requires;
  • to tell you about important changes to a product you use.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

Who we share with

We share information only where it is needed to provide the service:

  • service providers that host our software, deliver email, process payments or collect error reports, under their own confidentiality and security terms;
  • Meta and LinkedIn, when you connect an account or when we publish a post you approved;
  • authorities, where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting

We keep information for as long as you use the service or the project runs, and for as long afterwards as the law requires for accounts and tax records. When you disconnect an account or ask us to delete your data, we delete it as described on the data deletion page.

We protect information with access controls and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw a permission you gave. Email admin@busyfox.in from the address linked to your account and we will reply. You also have the rights given to you by Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Our products are for businesses and adults. They are not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant for a product you use, we will tell you in the product or by email.
