01 What it does

Nothing goes out until you have said yes.

  • Connects the Pages and accounts you choose, through a link we send you
  • Publishes the posts you approved to those accounts
  • Reads reach and engagement for what was published
  • Feeds the reports we send you

02 How connecting works

You connect through the platform, not through us.

1. You sign in

We send you a connect link. You sign in on the official Meta or LinkedIn screen. We never see or store your password.

2. You choose

That screen lists what Busy Fox Insights is asking for. You pick the Pages and accounts to share, and you can decline.

3. We receive a token

The platform gives Busy Fox Insights an access token limited to what you allowed. You can withdraw it at any time.

03 What we access and why

Only what the job needs.

  • What we accessWhy we need it
  • Your name, profile picture and the list of Pages and accounts you chooseTo show you which accounts are connected and to send posts to the right one.
  • Posts and media on the connected Pages and accountsTo show what is already published next to what is scheduled.
  • Permission to publish to those Pages and accountsTo publish the posts you approved, at the time you approved.
  • Performance figures such as reach, impressions, engagement and follower countsTo build the reports we send you.
  • Comments and messages on the connected accountsOnly if you ask us toTo reply to your customers for you.
  • Ad account dataOnly if you ask us toTo run and report on ads for you.

The exact permissions are listed on the Meta or LinkedIn consent screen when you connect.

04 What we do not do

The short list of nevers.

No posting without approval

We do not publish anything you have not approved.

No selling data

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it to advertise to you.

No passwords

Sign-in happens on the platform. We hold a token, never a password.

No extra reading

We use only the accounts you connected, for the purposes listed above.

05 How to disconnect

Leave whenever you like.

On the platform

Facebook: Settings, then Business integrations. Instagram: Settings, then Apps and websites. LinkedIn: Settings, then Data privacy, then Permitted services.

By email

Write to admin@busyfox.in. We disconnect the account and confirm by email.
